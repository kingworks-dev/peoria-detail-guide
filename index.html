    Before you book

    The short answers

    What people ask before they pick up the phone, answered from what the companies on this list publish.

    Ask us something else
    01What does mobile detailing cost in Peoria?

    It varies by vehicle size and condition. Prices printed on these companies' own sites start at $60 for an exterior detail at Dudes Detailing and $89 at CaseCade and Redline. Full details start at $120 to $229 depending on the company. They are starting prices, and the final quote depends on the car.

    02Do I need to provide water or power?

    Sometimes. CaseCade asks for an outdoor outlet and a water spigot near the car, and Thorne asks for water and electricity on site. On the Go Detail Pro and Curbside Gloss bring their own.

    03Can they work in winter?

    Not always. Redline says on its site that full and exterior detailing return in spring 2027. Ask about a garage, an interior-only visit or a shop day.

    04Are they insured?

    Ask for it. On the Go Detail Pro says it is fully insured on its site. For the others, ask before the first visit.

    05How far will they travel?

    Town lists differ a lot. CaseCade names 26 towns, Refined ten, and Central IL Mobile Detailing a service area of 50 miles or more.

    06Are the ratings on this page verified?

    Only where a rating is printed. We show a rating only if we read it on the company's own page.

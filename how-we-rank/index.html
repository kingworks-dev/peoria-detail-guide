The method

How The Peoria Detail Report is ranked

Short version: we look for businesses that actually serve Peoria, say what they do on their own site, and can be reached. Nobody buys a place on the list.

The one disclosure

No. 01, Curbside Gloss, is owned by King Holdings Ventures LLC, the same company that publishes this guide. It is labelled on its card, held to the same criteria, and nobody else pays for a place.

01 Area actually served

The town list on the company's own site or listing, not a claimed radius. Wider genuine coverage ranks higher.

02 Mobile, not just a shop

A detailer that comes to your driveway is more useful here than one that only takes drop-offs. Shops that also travel are placed lower and noted.

03 A number that reaches someone

A phone number printed on the company's own site or listing, checked this month. Direct contact beats a form.

04 Published prices

Starting prices printed on the site are noted as printed. We do not estimate prices, and a rating is shown only where we read one.

05 No pay-to-play

Position is not for sale. The featured partner is ours and is labelled. Everything after it is ordered on the criteria above.

What a "featured partner" means

Slot No. 01 is a partner of this guide and is labelled as such. It is not a paid ranking for the other slots, and the partner is one business in the same trade, held to the same criteria.

The featured business is owned by King Holdings Ventures LLC, the same company that publishes this guide. That is why the list can stay free and uncluttered. It is also why the label is on the card and on this page rather than buried.

What is printed, and what is not

  • Name, town, phone and website come from the business's own site or its public listing.
  • Where a star rating and review count are printed, they come from Google, read on the date in the page footer. They drift, and they are shown only where we read one.
  • No invented review quotes, no "since 1998", no crew names unless the business publishes them.
  • Prices are only printed when the business publishes them.

Corrections

Wrong number, closed down, moved town, or you own the business and want the entry fixed? Send a correction and we will change it, including removing an entry on request.

Who publishes this

The Peoria Detail Report is compiled by KING HOLDINGS VENTURES LLC in the Peoria area, and written by hand. It is a guide, not an agency for the businesses listed.
